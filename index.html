Hi, my name is

Koushik Bera.

I am a Senior Research Scholar in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Guwahati, working under the supervision of Prof. N. Selvaraju. Originally from Midnapore, West Bengal, my Ph.D. thesis focuses on statistical and deep learning approaches to volatility forecasting in financial time series.
