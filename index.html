STATISTICS & FINANCIAL ECONOMETRICS

Koushik Bera

I completed my PhD in Statistics in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, under the supervision of Prof. Selvaraju Natarajan. My doctoral research examined statistical and deep learning approaches to volatility forecasting across financial markets; I defended my thesis on 30 June 2026.

My research interests centre on time series forecasting using econometric and deep learning approaches, with particular emphasis on financial volatility and risk. My work combines state space models, decomposition methods and neural networks to analyse high-frequency financial data and develop forecasts for risk assessment and portfolio optimisation.

Research interests

My work sits at the intersection of statistics, financial econometrics and statistical learning.

I.

Volatility & risk

Volatility modelling, risk forecasting and the analysis of high-frequency financial data.

II.

Time series & learning

Time series analysis, state space methods and statistical machine learning for financial forecasting.

III.

Portfolio decisions

Portfolio optimisation and the role of volatility forecasts in financial decision-making.
